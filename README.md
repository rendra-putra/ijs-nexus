## 🇦🇺 Australia Awards Indonesia Initiative

**IJS Nexus Explorer** was conceptualized and developed by **Rendra A.A. Putra** as part of the **Australia Awards Indonesia Short Course: Leveraging Technology and Data for Justice and Security Sector Reform**, hosted by **Griffith University, Australia**.

The project demonstrates the potential of integrating **Big Data, Artificial Intelligence, Geospatial Intelligence, and Legal Knowledge Systems** to support evidence-based policymaking, improve transparency, and enhance access to justice information across Indonesia.

By bridging fragmented justice-sector data with socio-economic indicators and AI-powered legal assistance, IJS Nexus Explorer aims to contribute to a more inclusive, accountable, and data-driven justice ecosystem.

---

## 🌐 Overview

**IJS Nexus Explorer** is an integrated intelligence platform that combines justice-sector data, socio-economic indicators, geospatial analytics, and Artificial Intelligence into a single ecosystem.

The platform transforms fragmented datasets into meaningful insights through interactive maps, statistical analysis, and AI-powered legal assistance. By connecting justice and socio-economic information, IJS Nexus helps stakeholders make evidence-based decisions, improve transparency, and strengthen public understanding of Indonesia’s legal landscape.

---

## 🎯 Vision

To establish a transparent, inclusive, and data-driven justice ecosystem where every stakeholder can access, understand, and utilize justice information to improve governance, accountability, and societal outcomes.

## 🚀 Mission

To bridge the gap between complex justice-sector information and impactful decision-making by integrating geospatial analytics, socio-economic intelligence, Big Data, Machine Learning, and Generative AI into a unified platform.

Through IJS Nexus Explorer, we aim to:

- Democratize access to justice information.
- Enable evidence-based policy formulation.
- Strengthen transparency and accountability.
- Foster public engagement through accessible legal knowledge.
- Leverage AI and advanced analytics to uncover actionable insights from complex datasets.

# ✨ Core Features

## 🗺️ Geospatial Explorer

### Crime Hotspots & Regional Analysis

Visualize crime patterns across Indonesia through interactive geographic intelligence tools.

#### Key Capabilities

- Interactive crime mapping down to Kabupaten/Kota level.
- Crime hotspot visualization using heatmaps.
- Choropleth boundary analysis.
- Dynamic filtering by region, crime type, and period.
- Spatial trend analysis for policy development.

---

## 📊 Socio-Economic Correlation Analytics

### Understanding the Drivers Behind Crime

Explore relationships between crime and socio-economic conditions through statistical analysis.

#### Key Capabilities

- Compare crime rates with:
  - Population Density
  - Poverty Levels
  - Education Index
  - Employment Indicators
  - Income Per Capita
- Pearson Correlation Analysis.
- Interactive scatter plots.
- Regional benchmarking.
- Outlier detection and comparative insights.

---

## 🤖 Ask AI

### AI-Powered Legal Assistant

A conversational legal assistant designed to simplify and democratize access to Indonesian laws and regulations.

Built using **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)**, the assistant retrieves information directly from trusted legal sources and provides contextualized explanations.

#### Knowledge Sources

- KUHP
- KUHAP
- UU ITE
- National Regulations
- Government Publications
- Justice-Sector Reports

#### Key Capabilities

- Natural language legal search.
- Regulatory summarization.
- Legal concept explanation.
- Context-aware question answering.
- Source-grounded responses.
